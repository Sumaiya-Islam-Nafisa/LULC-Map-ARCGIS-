# 🌍 Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) of Maheshkhali Island

## 📌 Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze and classify the Land Use/Land Cover (LULC) of Maheshkhali Island, Bangladesh, using Landsat 8 imagery.

## 🗂️ Data Sources
- Landsat 8 OLI/TIRS imagery (USGS Earth Explorer)
- Administrative boundary of Maheshkhali 
- Software: ArcGIS 10.8

## 🛠️ Methodology
1. **Data Collection**  
   - Downloaded Landsat 8 OLI/TIRS image (year: 2024).  

2. **Preprocessing**  
   - Layer stacking  
   - Clipping to Maheshkhali boundary  
   - Radiometric and atmospheric correction (if needed)  

3. **Classification**  
   - Supervised classification (Maximum Likelihood method)  
   - Defined training samples for major classes  

4. **Accuracy Assessment**  
   - Validation using reference points  
   
## 🗺️ LULC Classes
- 🌾 Agriculture  
- 🌳 Forest / Vegetation  
- 🏘️ Settlement / Built-up  
- 🏖️ Bare Land  
- 🌊 Water Bodies  

## 📊 Results
- Classified map of Maheshkhali for 2024  
- Looking at the map, the most prominent land uses appear to be Hill Forest (Yellow) covering the large central/eastern part of the island, followed by extensive areas of Agriculture (Light Green), and significant presence of Salt Pan (Pink) areas, especially toward the coastlines and river banks. Mangrove Forests (Dark Green) and Water (Blue) are also visible, primarily along the island's edges and inlets. Building Areas (Beige) seem to be scattered and less dominant than the other categories.
